## 4. Return and Refund Policy

### 4.1 Standard Refund Window

Customers may request a full refund on any GigaCorp software product, subscription, or service within **30 days** of the initial purchase date. After 30 days, refunds are handled on a prorated basis for the remaining service period.

### 4.2 Hardware Returns

For hardware products, the return window is **15 days from delivery**. Hardware must be:
- Returned in original packaging with all accessories, cables, and documentation
- In like-new condition with no physical damage
- Free of custom modifications or alterations

A **15% restocking fee** applies to opened hardware returns. Unopened hardware in original sealed packaging receives a full refund.

### 4.3 Enterprise Contracts

Enterprise customers with annual contracts may cancel within the first **60 days** for a full refund. After 60 days, early termination fees apply:
- **Months 3–6:** 50% of remaining contract value
- **Months 7–9:** 25% of remaining contract value
- **Month 10 onwards:** No refund, but service continues through the end of the billing period

### 4.4 Refund Processing Time

Once the return is received and inspected, refunds are processed within the following timelines:

| Payment Method | Processing Time |
|:--------------|:----------------|
| Credit/Debit Card | 5–10 business days |
| PayPal | 3–5 business days |
| Wire Transfer | 7–14 business days |
| Account Credit | 1–2 business days |

Refunds are always issued to the original payment method unless other arrangements are explicitly made with our billing team.

### 4.5 Non-Refundable Items and Services

The following are not eligible for refund under any circumstances:
- Professional services consulting hours that have already been delivered
- Custom development or implementation work
- Third-party software licenses (resold through GigaCorp)
- Training course enrollments after the course start date
- Prepaid usage credits that have been partially consumed (over 10% usage)
- Domain name registrations
- SSL certificates after issuance

### 4.6 How to Request a Refund

To initiate a return or refund:
1. Log in to the **Customer Portal** at portal.gigacorp.com
2. Navigate to **Orders → Request Return**
3. Select the order and item(s) you wish to return
4. Provide a reason for the return
5. Submit the request — you will receive a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number within 24 hours
6. Ship the item(s) back using the prepaid return label provided

### 4.7 Refund Exceptions

- **Seasonal promotions:** Products purchased during Black Friday or Cyber Monday sales may have extended return windows (up to 45 days for software, 30 days for hardware).
- **Volume licensing:** Custom refund terms may apply to volume license agreements (100+ seats). Refer to your Volume License Agreement for details.
- **Education and non-profit discounts:** Refunds follow the standard policy unless specified otherwise in the discount agreement.

## 5. Cancellation Rules

### 5.1 Subscription Cancellation

Monthly subscriptions can be canceled at any time. The subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period. No partial refunds are issued for the remaining days in a monthly billing cycle.

### 5.2 Annual Subscription Cancellation

Annual subscriptions canceled within 30 days of renewal receive a full refund. After 30 days, the cancellation takes effect at the end of the annual term, and no refund is provided for the remaining months.

### 5.3 Service Cancellation Process

To cancel a subscription or service:
1. Log in to the **Customer Portal** at portal.gigacorp.com
2. Go to **Account Settings → Subscriptions**
3. Click "Cancel Subscription" next to the service you wish to cancel
4. Select a cancellation reason (optional but helpful)
5. Confirm the cancellation

For enterprise contracts, cancellations must be submitted in writing to your account manager or to contracts@gigacorp.com at least 30 days before the desired cancellation date.

### 5.4 What Happens After Cancellation

- Service access continues until the end of the current billing period
- Data is retained for **90 days** after cancellation (grace period for data export)
- After 90 days, all data is permanently deleted from GigaCorp systems
- You can reactivate a canceled subscription within the 90-day grace period without losing data
- Any outstanding balance must be paid before cancellation is finalized

### 5.5 Free Trial Cancellation

Free trials can be canceled at any time before the trial period ends. No payment information is required for GigaAnalytics trials. Cloud services trials require a payment method for verification, but you will not be charged if you cancel before the trial ends.

## 6. Exchange Procedures

### 6.1 Hardware Exchanges

Defective hardware received within the warranty period may be eligible for an **Advanced Replacement**:
1. Contact Customer Support to diagnose the issue
2. If the issue is confirmed as a manufacturing defect, an Advanced Replacement is authorized
3. GigaCorp ships the replacement unit immediately with a prepaid return label
4. You have **15 days** to return the defective unit
5. If the defective unit is not returned within 15 days, you will be charged the full retail price of the replacement

### 6.2 Size or Configuration Changes

If you ordered the wrong hardware configuration, you may exchange it within 15 days of delivery:
- **Different model (same product line):** No exchange fee, price difference applied
- **Upgrade to higher model:** Price difference charged
- **Downgrade to lower model:** Refund of price difference, minus 10% restocking fee

### 6.3 Software License Exchanges

- **License type change** (e.g., perpetual to subscription): Allowed within 30 days. Difference in price is charged or refunded.
- **License seat reallocation:** Software licenses can be reassigned to different users at any time by the account administrator. No fee applies.
- **Edition upgrade** (e.g., Standard to Enterprise): Allowed at any time. Difference in price is charged prorated for the remainder of the billing period.

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 13.1 Account and Login

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Visit portal.gigacorp.com and click "Forgot Password." Enter your registered email address. You will receive a password reset link that expires within 1 hour. If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

**Q: How do I change my email address?**
A: Log in to the Customer Portal, go to Account Settings → Personal Information, and update your email address. A verification email will be sent to the new address. Your old email remains active until verification is complete.

**Q: My account is locked. What should I do?**
A: Accounts are locked after 5 failed login attempts. Wait 15 minutes for the lock to automatically release, or reset your password. If the issue persists, contact Customer Support.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Contact Customer Support to request account deletion. There is a 90-day data retention period after closure during which you can reactivate the account. Export any data you wish to keep before requesting deletion.

### 13.2 Billing and Payments

**Q: Can I change my billing cycle?**
A: Yes. Go to Account Settings → Billing → Billing Cycle in the Customer Portal. Switching from monthly to annual saves 20%. Changes take effect at the start of the next billing period.

**Q: Why was I charged twice?**
A: This may happen if a previous payment failed and was re-attempted. Check your billing history in the Customer Portal. Duplicate charges are rare; if you see one, contact billing@gigacorp.com for immediate resolution.

**Q: Do you offer refunds for unused time?**
A: For monthly subscriptions, no partial refunds are given for unused days. For annual subscriptions canceled within 30 days of renewal, a full refund is issued. Enterprise contracts follow the terms in Section 4.3.

**Q: Can I get an invoice for my purchase?**
A: Yes. Invoices are available in the Customer Portal under Billing History. PDF copies can be downloaded for the last 24 months. For older invoices, contact billing@gigacorp.com.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and wire transfers (enterprise customers). See Section 7.1 for details.

### 13.3 Shipping and Orders

**Q: How long does shipping take?**
A: Domestic Standard shipping takes 5–8 business days, Express takes 2–3 business days, and Next-Day takes 1 business day. International shipping varies by region (7–18 business days). See Section 3 for complete details.

**Q: Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?**
A: If the order has not yet shipped, log in to the Customer Portal, go to Orders → Order History, and select "Edit Shipping Address." If the order has already shipped, contact Customer Support to attempt a carrier reroute (not guaranteed).

**Q: Do you ship to PO boxes?**
A: PO Box addresses are accepted for Standard shipping only. Express and Next-Day shipping require a physical street address.

**Q: What if my package is lost or damaged?**
A: Report lost or damaged shipments within 7 days of the expected delivery date via the Customer Portal or by contacting Customer Support. GigaCorp will ship a replacement at no cost after verification. See Section 3.6 for details.

**Q: Can I track my order?**
A: Yes. Tracking information is sent via email when the order ships. You can also track orders in real time through the Customer Portal under Orders → Order History. See Section 12.1 for details.

### 13.4 Returns and Refunds

**Q: What is your return policy?**
A: Software products can be refunded within 30 days of purchase. Hardware returns are accepted within 15 days of delivery. A 15% restocking fee applies to opened hardware. See Section 4 for full details.

**Q: How long do refunds take?**
A: Refunds to credit/debit cards take 5–10 business days, PayPal takes 3–5 business days, and wire transfers take 7–14 business days after the return is received and inspected.

**Q: Can I return a digital product?**
A: Software licenses and digital products can be refunded within 30 days of purchase, provided the license key has not been activated on more than one device.

**Q: How do I return a hardware product?**
